## Question

Which project should we build for our capstone: **College Lost and Found** or **ExpenseBuddy**?

## Why it matters

This decision determines the scope, timeline, and technical stack for the entire semester. Choosing the wrong option could lead to scope creep or a project that's too simple to demonstrate meaningful learning.

## Options considered

| Option | What it would look like | Main benefit | Main concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| College Lost and Found | Web app where students post lost/found items, filter by category/location, and contact matches. MVP: auth, post item, search, messaging, mark resolved. | Clear problem statement, high user relevance, natural stretch goals (image matching, heat maps), team consensus (4/4 support), low-medium technical risk | Scope creep—adding AI features too early; need to keep first version simple |
| ExpenseBuddy | Expense-splitting app for roommates/student groups. MVP: create expense, select payers, calculate balances, view settlement. | Simpler logic to test, predictable backend, clear success metrics, good contingency plan | Less unique than Lost & Found; risks feeling generic; medium technical complexity |

## Evidence

What did you check?

- Source, test, sketch, or walkthrough: Team planning discussion (Issue #3), five candidate ideas doc, team working agreement
- Evidence link or attachment: https://github.com/CapstoneDesign-Fall2026-UlsanCollege/Team_4_Capstone_project/issues/3
- What I observed: 
  - Both ideas have equally viable MVPs with manageable scope for 8 weeks
  - College Lost and Found has stronger team alignment and coach rating (3/4 for both, but more unique value)
  - ExpenseBuddy is a solid fallback if scope becomes a problem
  - Team concern: preventing feature creep and maintaining focus on MVP

## Recommendation

I recommend **College Lost and Found** because it has the strongest alignment with project goals—it solves a real student problem, demonstrates technical and UX competency through a focused MVP, has natural opportunities to show advanced features as stretch goals, and all team members are committed to building it.

## Tradeoff or remaining uncertainty

The main disadvantage is **scope management**. We must resist adding image matching, maps, or notifications in the first 6 weeks. Success depends on disciplined scoping and using test data rather than trying to attract real users early. ExpenseBuddy remains a solid backup if we hit unexpected technical blockers with Lost & Found.
