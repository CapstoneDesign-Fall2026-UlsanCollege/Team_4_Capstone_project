## Candidate vertical slice

- **User or actor: College student  
- **Start state: The student is not logged in and wants to find a campus activity 
- **Smallest end-to-end path:**
-  The student logs in.
- The system displays the student home page.
- The student selects Events.
- The system displays available campus events.
- The student selects an event.
- The system displays the event details.
- The student can save or join the activity. 
- **What the demo should prove: The app can take a student from login to discovering and viewing a useful campus activity through a simple end-to-end flow.  
- **What is deliberately out of scope: AI-based recommendations, real-time messaging, payment features, complex social features, and advanced image-based matching.
