# Five Project Ideas

Team: [teaM_4]
Week: 1


## Idea 1 
**Project name:** CampusVibes
**Target user:** College students looking for events, clubs, and activities
**Problem:** Students miss out on relevant events/clubs because there's no centralized discovery platform; manually scrolling bulletin boards is inefficient
**Smallest useful version:** Web app that scrapes campus event calendars + clubs directory, displays events filtered by interests (music, sports, tech, etc.)
**Midterm demo could show:** Personalized recommendations based on past event attendance; event calendar with RSVP feature
**Big risk / unknown:** Getting access to all event data; accurate interest profiling

## Idea 2
**Project name:** College Lost and Found
**Target user:** Students, faculty, and college staff members
**Problem:** Students frequently lose items on college but there is no centralized system for reporting and finding them.
**Smallest useful version:** A mobile-friendly web app allowing users to post lost/found listings with photos, tags, and location zones.
**Midterm demo could show:** An interactive heat map of busy ares where items are frequently lost, alogside basic on user-generated text and images.
**Big risk / unknown:** It may be diffult to automatically deterine whether two items are the same.

Idea 3
Project name: CampusFood
Target user: College students looking for affordable and convenient food options around campus
Problem: Students don't know what restaurants and food options are available around campus, including their menus, prices, and food categories
Smallest useful version: Web app that displays nearby restaurants and their menus, allowing students to search and filter food by restaurant, category, and price
Midterm demo could show: Restaurant list; menu and food prices; search and category filters; restaurant details including location and opening hours
Big risk / unknown: Keeping restaurant and menu information accurate and up to date

##Idea 4
**Project name:** MediMate
**Target user:** Foreign students and foreigners who have difficulty communicating in Korean at hospitals
**Problem:** Many foreign patients cannot speak Korean well, making it difficult to communicate with doctors, nurses, and hospital staff.
**Smallest useful version:** Mobile app where users select their language and find other foreign people or volunteers who can help with basic communication at hospitals.
**Midterm demo could show:** Language-based search, helper profiles, hospital selection, help requests, and simple chat/translation features.
**Big risk / unknown:** Finding enough reliable language helpers and protecting users' privacy.

##Idea 5
**Project name:**ExpenseBuddy
**Target user:** Students living with roommates/friends
**Problem:** Friends have difficulty tracking who paid for shared expenses.
**Smallest useful version:** Add an expense → select people → app calculates how much each person owes.
**Midterm demo could show:** Monthly expense charts, payment history, and automatic settlement calculations.
**Big risk / unknown:** Handling complicated splitting situations.
